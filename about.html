About

What is this site?

NewYorkTurkEats is a fan-made, unofficial directory of every restaurant featured by NewYorkTurk (Ertan Bek), the New York City content creator known for surprising struggling, family-run restaurants with glowing, incognito reviews. This site exists to make that growing list easy to browse, search, and map.

It is not run by, affiliated with, or endorsed by NewYorkTurk. Think of it as a companion index for fans who want to find a restaurant they saw in a video, see what's nearby on the map, or track how the list has grown over time.
